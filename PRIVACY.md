# Privacy Policy – RSI AUDifier

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

RSI AUDifier does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal information.

- The extension runs only on robertsspaceindustries.com. It reads price text on the page locally in your browser in order to display an AUD equivalent. That page content is never sent anywhere.
- To get the exchange rate, the extension requests the USD→AUD rate from open.er-api.com (and frankfurter.app as a fallback). These requests contain no information about you or the pages you visit, though those services will see your IP address as any website would.
- The most recent exchange rate and its timestamp are cached locally using Chrome's extension storage. Nothing else is stored.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.

RSI AUDifier is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Cloud Imperium Games or Roberts Space Industries.
