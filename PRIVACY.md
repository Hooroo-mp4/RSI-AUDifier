# Privacy Policy – RSI Utilify

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

RSI Utilify does not collect, store on any server, transmit or sell any personal information.

- The extension runs only on robertsspaceindustries.com. It reads price text and RSI's own stylesheets on the page locally in your browser, in order to show converted prices and to recolour the site. That page content is never sent anywhere.
- To recolour the site's blue background images, the extension downloads those images from RSI's own site and CDN (robertsspaceindustries.com and cdn.robertsspaceindustries.com), recolours them on your device, and keeps the recoloured copies locally. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- To get exchange rates, the extension requests USD rates from open.er-api.com (and frankfurter.app as a fallback). These requests contain no information about you or the pages you visit, though those services will see your IP address as any website would.
- The following is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's extension storage, and nowhere else: the most recent exchange rates and their timestamp, your settings (the "Always show converted prices" toggle, your chosen currency and your chosen theme), a cached copy of the theme styles generated on your device so the theme loads quickly, and the recoloured background images (also generated on your device).
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.

RSI Utilify is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Cloud Imperium Games or Roberts Space Industries.
